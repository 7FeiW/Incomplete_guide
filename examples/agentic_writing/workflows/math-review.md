# Review Mathematical Content

Read the [review contract](review.md), target text, notation and definitions,
relevant equations, and the supplied derivations or result records.

Check notation consistency, declared domains and assumptions, units or
dimensions where applicable, equation references, and whether transformations
or approximations state their conditions. Compare numerical claims with the
provided result records when they are available.

Return read-only findings in the shared format. Distinguish an algebraic or
notation issue from a question that needs author or domain-expert judgment. Do
not invent a derivation, silently correct equations, or claim numerical results
were recomputed unless that computation was requested and performed.
