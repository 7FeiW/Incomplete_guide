# Review Writing

Read the [review contract](review.md), target text, audience, terminology, and
relevant parts of [the style profile](../style.md).

Check paragraph purpose, progression, transitions, ambiguous pronouns, undefined
terms, repetition, and sentences that obscure an operation or scientific point.
Preserve necessary technical detail and hedging. Judge clarity for the audience,
without enforcing sentence or paragraph lengths.

Return located, read-only findings and proposed actions. Keep scientific
questions separate; flag any clarification requiring a scientific choice.
