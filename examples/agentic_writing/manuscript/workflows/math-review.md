# Review Mathematical Content

Read the [review contract](review.md), target text, the
[notation register](../notation.md), relevant equations, and the supplied
derivations or result records.

Check the draft against the register for symbol and abbreviation consistency,
and report symbols the register does not define. Check declared domains and
assumptions, units or dimensions where applicable, equation references, and
whether transformations or approximations state their conditions. Compare
numerical claims with the provided result records when they are available.

Return read-only findings in the shared format. Distinguish an algebraic or
notation issue from a question that needs author or domain-expert judgment. Do
not invent a derivation, silently correct equations, or claim numerical results
were recomputed unless that computation was requested and performed.
