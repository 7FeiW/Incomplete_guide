# Review Mathematical Proofs

Read the [review contract](review.md), target proof, theorem or proposition
statement, definitions, notation, and cited prerequisites. Read the proof in
the source format used by the manuscript.

Check that each inference follows from stated assumptions or an identified
result; that quantifiers, domains, and edge cases are handled; and that the
conclusion is the one stated. Identify missing definitions, unsupported steps,
circular reasoning, and steps whose validity depends on an unavailable source.

Return read-only findings in the shared format. Give the location, the required
assumption or justification, and the consequence of each issue. Do not fill a
gap with a new proof, assert that a proof is correct without checking its
steps, or replace author review by a formal proof checker.
