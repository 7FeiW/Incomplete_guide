# Review Scientific Logic

Read the [review contract](review.md), target text, research question, outline,
and relevant claim and experiment records.

Check explicit premises, inference steps, consistent definitions, contradictions,
circular reasoning, comparison axes, and generalization beyond evaluated data.
Distinguish correlation and causation; assess controls, confounders, alternative
explanations, and limitations.

Return read-only findings in the shared format. Explain reasoning problems
without inventing missing premises or substituting grammar feedback. Separate
unsupported inferences from questions that need additional evidence.
