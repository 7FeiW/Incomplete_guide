# Review or Revise Author Style

Read the [review contract](review.md), target text, [style profile](../style.md),
and only permitted, relevant samples. Report unavailable samples. Accuracy and
venue requirements take precedence over style preferences.

## Review

Return located suggestions for generic phrasing, inflated language, repetitive
transitions, unnecessary formality, and departures from the agreed voice.
Preserve technically necessary wording. Do not edit unless requested.

## Requested Revision

After substantive scientific questions are resolved, make small changes within
the named scope. Preserve numbers, units, equations, citation keys, labels,
negation, causal language, scope, and uncertainty. Do not copy sample sentences,
import their facts, or add anecdotes, mistakes, or opinions to appear human.

Compare the diff with the original, flag meaning-sensitive changes, and report
checks performed and unresolved decisions. Evaluate readability, author voice,
and meaning preservation; detector scores do not establish authorship or accuracy.
