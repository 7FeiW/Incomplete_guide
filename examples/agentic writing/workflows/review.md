# Coordinate Manuscript Reviews

## Shared Contract

Read repository instructions. Identify target text, draft revision including
uncommitted content, research question, evidence, and requested roles. Request
an unclear target; report missing context. Reviewers assess the same unchanged
draft without editing it. Use separate reports for concurrent reviews.

## Findings

For each finding, report:
- Role and precise manuscript location with a short identifying excerpt.
- Priority: consequential scientific issue, clarity issue, or optional preference.
- Assessment: demonstrated issue, unresolved question, or suggestion.
- Problem, consequence, supporting evidence or missing context, and next action.

Report draft version, inputs actually read, checks performed, and unassessed
scope. No findings is not certification of correctness.

## Handoff

Consolidate duplicate findings, retain disagreements, and decide from evidence
and author preferences rather than reviewer votes. The author resolves scientific
questions and selects revisions. One editor applies them, followed by the style
pass and checks of changed claims and meaning. Record dispositions, validation,
and remaining gaps in the task record. Sequential passes are suitable for small
tasks; separate agents are optional.
